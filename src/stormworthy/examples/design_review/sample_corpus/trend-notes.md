# Dashboard trend notes (reference channel — inspiration, NOT specification)

Mood-board material. Nothing in this file is a requirement, a measurement, or a standard; it
exists to show how a low-trust `reference` channel behaves in the gate.

Dashboards this year lean dark-canvas with one saturated accent, oversized hero metrics, and
sparklines everywhere. Bento grids are still popular; so are glassy translucent cards, though
they age quickly. Motion is calmer than last year — micro-transitions over hero animations.

If a claim cites only this file, that is a signal the panel is decorating, not verifying.
